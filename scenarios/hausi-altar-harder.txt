#! nostage
# Hausi - offering at a boss's altar in that biome's sash. NEEDS a Vandi with AskHarder and
# IsHarderSummon. hausi-no-vandi is the same altar without them.
#
# `appears` and not a fixed wait: this altar takes 12s by its own account, and a game running slowly in
# the background took longer, which read as the mod not summoning.
#
# Every count here looks 80 m out. The altar puts its boss up to 40 m from its spawn point, and a
# check at the default 20 m read "no relic" for a relic lying 30 m away.
#
# The one relic is checked on the ground, after the boss's HardAntler has landed: a ragdoll spills its
# loot seconds after the death, and a count taken before that reads 0 whether a relic is coming or not.
# The rounds wanting none read `relics=` instead, which the kill has already settled by the time `kill`
# returns. The ground cannot answer those: sweep stops short of 80 m, so round one's relic is still lying
# there, and a count of 0 before the spill would pass for the wrong reason anyway.
#
# What it proves: the boss comes one star above the record, because Vandi was asked and Vandi's stars
# are the only difficulty there is. It drops one relic, not doubled by the very sash that asked for
# it. The sash is not consumed. An offering without the sash, or in the wrong biome's, asks for
# nothing and gets no relic, and the record is still Vandi's own.

clear
god
ghost
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr
unkey vandi_harder_defeated_eikthyr
wait 1
# In the Meadows, because Utangard refuses this altar anywhere the group has not earned, and the file
# before this one may have left the character anywhere.
biome Meadows
wait 3
location Eikthyrnir
wait 3
banish Eikthyr 80
sweep

# In the sash, no kills on record: a plain Eikthyr would be level 1.
give HausiSash_Meadows:1
give TrophyDeer:2
mod hausi wear HausiSash_Meadows
wait 2
mod hausi stats reset
offer TrophyDeer
appears Eikthyr 90 80
wait 3
level Eikthyr 2
carrying HausiSash_Meadows 1
kill Eikthyr
mod hausi floor HardAntler 80
until 45 any=yes
mod hausi floor HausiRelic_Meadows 80
until 10 total=1
mod hausi floor TrophyEikthyr 80
printed total=0 yes
mod hausi last
printed last=Eikthyr yes
printed chance=0.5 yes
keyis vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr 1
sweep
strip HausiRelic_Meadows,HardAntler,TrophyEikthyr
wait 1

# Taken off: one kill on record is one star, level 2, and no relic.
mod hausi wear HausiSash_Meadows
wait 2
give TrophyDeer:2
offer TrophyDeer
appears Eikthyr 90 80
wait 3
level Eikthyr 2
kill Eikthyr
mod hausi stats
printed relics=1 yes
keyis vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr 2
sweep
strip HardAntler,TrophyEikthyr
wait 1

# The wrong biome's sash asks for nothing: two kills is two stars, level 3, and no relic of any kind.
give HausiSash_Swamp:1
give TrophyDeer:2
mod hausi wear HausiSash_Swamp
wait 2
offer TrophyDeer
appears Eikthyr 90 80
wait 3
level Eikthyr 3
kill Eikthyr
mod hausi stats
printed relics=1 yes
sweep
mod hausi wear HausiSash_Swamp
wait 2
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr
clear

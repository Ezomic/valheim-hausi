# Hausi - death while wearing. The trophy goes to the grave with everything else: a mark left on it
# would keep it in the pack, because MoveInventoryToGrave leaves m_equipped items behind.

clear
god off
ghost off
give TrophyBoar:3
wait 1
mod hausi wear TrophyBoar
wait 2
mod hausi
printed worn=TrophyBoar yes
die
wait 12
mod hausi
printed worn=none yes
printed published=no yes
carrying TrophyBoar 0
god
ghost

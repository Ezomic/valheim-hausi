# Hausi - the Deep North sash (LHM-98): six trophies and the Frozen King's Sacrificial Blood in place of a
# boss trophy he does not drop, refused without the blood, made with it, worn at the middle tier, upgraded
# with its relic and Moose hide, and then a true double whose token glows.
#
# The set line is read first and in full, because the recipe is whatever the tables say: a Deep North
# trophy gained or lost fails here and not as a confusing refusal at the bench.
#
# The glow is checked through `hausi look`, which reports what this machine actually drew on the worn
# model, driven off the wearer's own ZDO quality: none at quality 1, the biome's colour at quality 2.
# paired-hausi-glow-* is the same from another player's machine.

clear
god
ghost
mod hausi sets
printed set.deepnorth boss=FrozenKingDrop count=6 trophies=TrophyBarka,TrophyElaking,TrophyJotunWarrior,TrophyJotunWitch,TrophyMoose,TrophySeal yes

place wood_beam 3.2 1.0 5 0.01
place wood_beam 3.4 -1.0 -174 0.00
place wood_floor 4.2 1.1 -174 0.02
place wood_floor 4.4 -0.9 -174 0.03
place wood_beam 4.1 2.1 -84 0.00
place wood_beam 4.5 -1.9 -84 0.03
place wood_beam 5.2 1.2 -174 0.00
place wood_beam 5.4 -0.8 -174 0.06
place piece_workbench 4.6 0.1 -174 0.10
place wood_pole2 3.1 2.0 5 1.04
place wood_pole2 3.5 -2.0 5 1.00
place woodwall 4.1 2.1 96 1.00
place woodwall 4.5 -1.9 -84 1.03
place woodwall 5.2 1.2 5 1.00
place woodwall 5.4 -0.8 5 1.06
place wood_pole2 5.1 2.2 5 1.01
place wood_pole2 5.5 -1.8 5 1.07
place wood_roof_ocorner_45 4.2 1.1 5 2.02
place wood_roof_ocorner_45 4.4 -0.9 -84 2.03
place wood_beam 4.1 2.1 95 2.00
place wood_beam 4.5 -1.9 -84 2.03
place wood_beam 5.2 1.2 5 2.00
place wood_beam 5.4 -0.8 5 2.06
place wood_beam_45 3.2 1.0 -174 3.01
place wood_beam_45 3.4 -1.0 6 3.00
place piece_workbench_ext1 7.2 0.2 -90 0
wait 2
goto piece_workbench
give TrophyBarka:1,TrophyElaking:1,TrophyJotunWarrior:1,TrophyJotunWitch:1,TrophyMoose:1,TrophySeal:1
wait 1
use piece_workbench
wait 1

# Every trophy and no blood: the stand-in is the last ingredient, as a boss trophy is elsewhere.
craftable HausiSash_DeepNorth no
close
wait 1

give FrozenKingDrop:1
wait 1
use piece_workbench
wait 2
craftable HausiSash_DeepNorth yes
craft HausiSash_DeepNorth
wait 5
carrying HausiSash_DeepNorth 1
carrying FrozenKingDrop 0
carrying TrophyBarka 0
carrying TrophyElaking 0
carrying TrophyJotunWarrior 0
carrying TrophyJotunWitch 0
carrying TrophyMoose 0
carrying TrophySeal 0
close
wait 1

# Quality 1: the middle tier, and no glow.
mod hausi wear HausiSash_DeepNorth
wait 2
mod hausi
printed worn=HausiSash_DeepNorth yes
printed biome=deepnorth yes
printed published=yes yes
mod hausi look
printed look.self=Spine2 yes
printed glow.self=none yes
sweep
spawn Moose
kill Moose 12
wait 6
mod hausi last
printed last=Moose yes
printed creaturebiome=deepnorth yes
printed chance=0.5 yes
dropped TrophyMoose 0
sweep

# The upgrade, off the body, with nothing but what it costs.
mod hausi wear HausiSash_DeepNorth
wait 2
give HausiRelic_DeepNorth:1
give MooseHide:10
wait 1
use piece_workbench
wait 2
press Upgrade
wait 2
craft HausiSash_DeepNorth
wait 5
close
wait 1
carrying HausiRelic_DeepNorth 0
carrying MooseHide 0
carrying HausiSash_DeepNorth 1

# Quality 2: the true double, and the token glows in the Deep North's colour.
mod hausi wear HausiSash_DeepNorth
wait 2
mod hausi look
printed glow.self=#1D4353 yes
screenshot
sweep
spawn Moose
kill Moose 12
wait 6
mod hausi last
printed last=Moose yes
printed chance=1 yes
dropped TrophyMoose 0
sweep

# Off again: the glow goes with the model.
mod hausi wear HausiSash_DeepNorth
wait 2
mod hausi look
printed look.self=none yes
goto piece_workbench

# The window remembers its tab across scenarios, and on the Upgrade tab its big button also reads
# Upgrade, so the next file's `press Upgrade` would press that instead of the tab.
use piece_workbench
wait 1
press Craft
wait 1
close
clear

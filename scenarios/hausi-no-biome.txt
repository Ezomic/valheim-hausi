# Hausi - no biome on either side. A Greyling drops no trophy, so it has no biome, and a matching
# trophy gets nothing from it. And `none` must not match `none`: a trophy the tables cannot place,
# worn against a creature they cannot place, is still no bonus. TrophySkeletonHildir is one of the
# location-only trophies, listed by `hausi sets` under nobiome.

clear
god
ghost
give TrophyBoar:1
give TrophySkeletonHildir:1
mod hausi wear TrophyBoar
wait 2
sweep
mod hausi stats reset
spawn Greyling
kill Greyling 12
wait 6
mod hausi stats
printed units=0 yes
mod hausi last
printed last=Greyling yes
printed creaturebiome=none yes
printed chance=0 yes
printed blocked=none yes

mod hausi wear TrophySkeletonHildir
wait 2
mod hausi
printed worn=TrophySkeletonHildir yes
printed biome=none yes
spawn Greyling
kill Greyling 12
wait 6
mod hausi stats
printed units=0 yes
mod hausi last
printed wornbiome=none yes
printed creaturebiome=none yes
printed chance=0 yes
spawn Boar
kill Boar 12
wait 6
mod hausi stats
printed units=0 yes
mod hausi last
printed last=Boar yes
printed wornbiome=none yes
printed blocked=none yes
sweep
mod hausi wear TrophySkeletonHildir
wait 2

#! nostage
# Hausi - the Queen's altar, in the Mistlands sash. NEEDS a Vandi with AskHarder and IsHarderSummon.
#
# The room dvergr_new_bossroom_ENTRANCE02 holds a real OfferingBowl, offeraltar_queen: SeekerQueen for 3
# TrophySeekerBrute, four spawn points, read off the prefab on game 1.0.21. She stands there the first
# time, and this is how she comes back. So the sash's altar rule covers her with nothing added, and
# this file is the proof, Utangard included: an offering it refuses must cost nothing and ask nothing.
#
# Every reach here is 5100 m because the room is not near anything you can see: the dungeon generates at
# y 5066 above its entrance, with the bowl live and the player standing on the surface. A hand step
# covers standing in the room.
#
# The world's own Queen is banished first, so that the one measured is the one this file summoned.

clear
god
ghost
unkey defeated_goblinking
unkey utangard_open_defeated_goblinking
unkey wither_open_defeated_goblinking
unkey utangard_first_defeated_goblinking
unkey utangard_p_$player_defeated_goblinking
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_queen
unkey vandi_harder_defeated_queen
wait 1
biome Mistlands
wait 3
location Mistlands_DvergrBossEntrance1 30
wait 5
banish SeekerQueen 5100
give HausiSash_Mistlands:1
give TrophySeekerBrute:9
mod hausi wear HausiSash_Mistlands
wait 2
mod hausi stats reset

# The group has not earned the Mistlands: Utangard refuses, nothing is taken, nothing is asked for.
centre The land will not answer no
offer TrophySeekerBrute 5100
centre The land will not answer an offering here yes
wait 15
count SeekerQueen 0 5100
carrying TrophySeekerBrute 9

# Earned, with the sash off. Nothing is on record, so she comes plain, and level 1 here is also what
# proves the refused offering above asked for nothing: an ask left over would make her level 2.
key utangard_open_defeated_goblinking
wait 1
mod hausi wear HausiSash_Mistlands
wait 2
offer TrophySeekerBrute 5100
centre The land will not answer no
appears SeekerQueen 90 5100
wait 3
level SeekerQueen 1 5100
kill SeekerQueen 5100
mod hausi stats
printed relics=0 yes
keyis vandi_p_$player_defeated_queen 1

# In the sash: one kill on record is one star, and the sash is one more, level 3.
mod hausi wear HausiSash_Mistlands
wait 2
offer TrophySeekerBrute 5100
appears SeekerQueen 90 5100
wait 3
level SeekerQueen 3 5100
carrying HausiSash_Mistlands 1
carrying TrophySeekerBrute 3
kill SeekerQueen 5100
mod hausi stats
printed relics=1 yes
mod hausi last
printed last=SeekerQueen yes
printed creaturebiome=mistlands yes
printed chance=0.5 yes
keyis vandi_p_$player_defeated_queen 2
mod hausi floor HausiRelic_Mistlands 5100
until 10 total=1
sweep
mod hausi wear HausiSash_Mistlands
wait 2
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_queen
unkey utangard_open_defeated_goblinking
clear

# Hausi - what is worn comes back after a load. `hausi reload` writes the pack out through the real save
# format, reads it back, and calls EquipInventoryItems, in the order Player.Load does. A real restart is
# in the hand steps: a scenario cannot survive one, and a -devkit-quit run leaves no inventory on disk,
# which was compared with Hausi removed and is the same there.
clear
god
ghost
give TrophyDeer:3
mod hausi wear TrophyDeer
wait 2
mod hausi
printed worn=TrophyDeer yes
mod hausi reload
wait 2
carrying TrophyDeer 3
mod hausi
printed worn=TrophyDeer yes
printed stack=3 yes
printed marks=1 yes
printed published=yes yes
mod hausi look
printed look.self=Hips yes
mod hausi wear TrophyDeer
wait 2
clear

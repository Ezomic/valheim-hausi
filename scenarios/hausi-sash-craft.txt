# Hausi - the sash from nothing: refused with a trophy missing, made from the whole set, worn,
# upgraded with its relic and material, and then a true double.
#
# Meadows, because its set is the smallest and is the one written here: Boar, Deer, Neck and Eikthyr.
# Reading them off `hausi sets` first means a change in what the tables say fails at the first step
# rather than as a confusing refusal at the bench.
#
# The double is checked on the ground as well as in the ledger. At a chance of 1 every unit gets one
# more, so the ground holds exactly what the ledger totals, and extra equals base for every item.

clear
god
ghost
mod hausi sets
printed set.meadows boss=TrophyEikthyr count=4 trophies=TrophyBoar,TrophyDeer,TrophyEikthyr,TrophyNeck yes

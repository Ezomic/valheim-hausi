# before this one may have left the character anywhere.

wait 3
banish Eikthyr 80

wait 1
mod hausi wear TrophyDeer
wait 2
mod hausi
printed worn=TrophyDeer yes
offer TrophyDeer
wait 2
carrying TrophyDeer 0
mod hausi
printed worn=none yes
printed published=no yes
printed marks=0 yes
wait 18
banish Eikthyr 80
